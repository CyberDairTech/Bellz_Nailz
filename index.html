Why Isabella’s nails last

Nobody wants a nail flying off mid-practice. Isabella uses Gel X tips and gel polish and builds each set to fit the person wearing it, so they sit flat and don’t lift easily. Sturdy, durable and really cute is the whole point.

She started making nails in 2023 and hasn’t stopped. She also posts them on YouTube, so you can watch the process.

How do I apply them?

Wipe your nails with the alcohol pad, gently buff, push back your cuticles, then add glue and press each nail on and hold for a few seconds. Easy.

Why do you need a photo of my hand?

Because every set is built to fit. A photo of one hand with all fingers showing next to a quarter lets Isabella size your nails accurately. It’s required for every order, custom or not.

Hi, I’m Isabella.

Okay, so confession: I saw my friend doing her nails and I was instantly obsessed. Like, I couldn’t stop thinking about it. That was in 2023, and I’ve been making nails ever since.

I’m really picky about one thing: my nails do NOT lift easily. I want them sturdy, durable, and really cute, and I build every set by hand so it fits you.

When I’m not doing nails, I’m doing volleyball or gymnastics, which is honestly why I care so much about nails that stay on. I also started a YouTube channel just for fun to show off my nails, and it kind of grew from there.

My big goal is to go to cosmetology school someday. Every set I sell gets me closer, so thank you for buying from me. It means so much!
