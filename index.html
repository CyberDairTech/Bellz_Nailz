How to apply

Six easy steps. Everything you need is already in your box.

1

Wash & dry

Clean, dry hands and nails. No lotion, please.

2

Push back cuticles

Use the little cuticle pusher to gently clear the nail.

3

Buff it

A quick buff gives the glue something to grip.

4

Wipe it down

Swipe the alcohol pad over each nail to remove oils.

5

Glue & press

Add a little glue, line up with your cuticle and press and hold.

6

Show them off

You did it! Salon looking nails in minutes.

Tip: Press firmly and try to keep your nails dry for an hour so they hold on longer.

Aftercare

Keep them happy

Wear gloves for dishes and cleaning, and don’t use your nails as tools or bottle openers.

Taking them off

Soak your fingertips in warm soapy water and ease the nails off. Please don’t peel or pull them.

Reuse them

Store your set in the case they came in so they stay safe for next time.
